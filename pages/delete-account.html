Delete Your Halalify Account

Delete from the app

You can delete your Halalify account directly in the app:

Settings > Contributor profile > Delete account

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by email:

Email: qias.std@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my Halalify account

What data is deleted

We delete the account profile and active session data associated with your account.

Data that may be retained

Contribution records may be retained in anonymized form for moderation and audit integrity.
