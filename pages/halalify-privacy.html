Privacy Policy – Halalify Halalify uses the camera to scan product barcodes and to let users submit product photos in contributions. We collect: - Email address (account creation/login) - User-submitted photos (product contribution) - User-submitted text content (reports/corrections) Purpose: - Provide app functionality (scan, account, moderation workflow) - Security and abuse prevention Data sharing: - We do not sell user data. - We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising. Security: - Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Retention and deletion: - Users can delete their account in-app: Settings > Contributor profile > Delete account. - Deletion requests can also be sent to: support@isnadware.com
